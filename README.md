Hi, I'm Wenxuan Wang, an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science in the ACM Honors Class at Zhiyuan College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Having completed my first year of study, I am particularly interested in the evolving relationship between humans and artificial intelligence—what each can do, how their roles may change, and how advances in AI may shape our future. I enjoy turning these broad questions into concrete problems and exploring possible answers through continuous learning and hands-on practice.

My current academic interests lie primarily in AI for Science and at the intersection of computer systems and chip design, particularly hardware–software co-design. I closely follow recent developments in these fields while continuing to build the mathematical, systems, and hardware foundations needed to explore them more deeply.

Outside academics, I am passionate about sports. I enjoy running, basketball, football, and tennis. I look forward to continuing to learn, embracing new challenges, and growing into a better version of myself.
